Map display

Select a state or territory on the map to view its population, annual change and growth rate. Zoom and pan to explore.

Population (people)

National context

27.61 million
Australia's estimated resident population at 30 June 2025.

The largest state populations are in New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland. Western Australia recorded the fastest annual growth among states and territories (2.2%).

How to use this map

• Change the mapped indicator using the menu.
• Click a state or territory for its data.
• Use the layer control to change the basemap.
• Use the + / − controls or mouse wheel to zoom.

Data: Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), National, state and territory population, June 2025, released 18 December 2025. ERP values are rounded to the nearest 100 people in the source table.

Boundaries: ABS Australian Statistical Geography Standard (ASGS) Edition 3, 2021 state/territory polygons, served via ArcGIS Feature Layer. Boundaries are statistical/cartographic, not legal boundaries.

Basemaps: Esri World Topographic / OpenStreetMap. Map prepared as an independent GEOM7200 Practical 8-style demonstration; not an official UQ course web map.
